What is it?
A percent means "out of 100". It is a way to compare amounts as parts of a hundred, so 25% means 25 out of every 100.
Key rules
- Percent of a number equals (percent / 100) x number.
- A percent increase or decrease applies to the original amount.
- To find what percent A is of B, divide A by B and multiply by 100.
Worked examples
Example 1: Find 20% of 150
1. Convert the percent: 20 / 100 = 0.2
2. Multiply: 0.2 x 150
3. Calculate the result: 30
Answer: 30
Example 2: Increase 50 by 10%
1. Find 10% of 50: 0.1 x 50 = 5
2. Add it to the original amount: 50 + 5
3. Check: 55 is 110% of 50
Answer: 55
Common mistake
Applying the percentage to the wrong base, or treating a 10% rise followed by a 10% fall as no change. The fall is 10% of the higher amount, so you end up at 99, not 100.
